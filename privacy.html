Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate this website:

  • Contact Form: Name, email, and message when you voluntarily submit our contact form.
  • Newsletter: Email address when you subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Login: Username for accessing restricted team resources (stored locally in your browser).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Send newsletter updates (with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your preferences:

  • Theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Accessibility settings (high contrast, text size)
  • Login session state

We do not use tracking cookies. No data is shared with third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

Our site may include links to or embeds from:

  • Google Fonts — for typography
  • Givebutter — for donation processing
  • Noupe — for FAQ bot functionality
  • RobotEvents — for competition information

Each service has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Login credentials are stored locally in your browser and are not transmitted to any server.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.
