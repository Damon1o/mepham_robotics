Logo Usage

The Mepham Robotics logo should always:

  • Appear with sufficient clear space around it
  • Not be stretched, skewed, or distorted
  • Use the official maroon/gold colors on light backgrounds
  • Use white/gold on dark backgrounds
  • Maintain a minimum size of 40px height for digital use

Don'ts

  • Don't use off-brand colors (no bright red, no neon green)
  • Don't mix fonts — stick to Balsamiq Sans and Space Mono
  • Don't use the logo as a watermark over photos
  • Don't recreate the logo — always use the official file
