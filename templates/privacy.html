Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what the site needs to work:

  • Contact form: the name, email address, message, and topic you submit. We also record the IP address and browser user-agent string of the submission, which we use to rate-limit spam.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Member accounts: username, email address, name, role, and a salted bcrypt hash of your password. We never store your password itself. If you turn on two-step sign-in we also keep the secret your authenticator app uses and hashed backup codes.
  • Signed-in devices: for each place you are signed in, the browser's user-agent string, the IP address, and when it signed in and was last active, so you can review and sign out devices on your account page.
  • Member pages and photos: anything you add to your own page (photo, headline, bio, skills, links, honours and pictures). Your page is visible to signed-in members only unless you choose to make it public.
  • Club activity: meeting sign-in and sign-out times, your answers to event invitations, the sign-up spots you take, and which guide chapters and resource links you have marked.
  • Discord: if you link your Discord account, its ID, username and display name, so the club bot knows which member is using it. We ask Discord for nothing else.
  • Assistant chat: the message you type is sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. We do not store the conversation.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To reply to your enquiries
  • To send newsletter updates you asked for
  • To limit spam and abusive traffic
  • To sign members in to restricted team pages
  • To run the club: attendance, event planning, volunteer sign-ups and team rosters

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We set one cookie: a signed session cookie that keeps you signed in and carries the token that protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and over HTTPS it is also marked Secure.

We use browser local storage for settings that stay on your device: your colour theme, the reduced-motion switch, the calendar filter and zoom, a dismissed announcement, your place in the guided tour, and scores in the game on the "page not found" page. None of it is sent to us.

While a tab is open, your conversation with the on-site assistant is kept in that tab's session storage so it follows you between pages, and its last few messages are sent along with each new question. Closing the tab clears it.

We use no tracking or advertising cookies and no analytics scripts.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Fonts — typography
  • Givebutter — donation processing, embedded on the support page
  • RobotEvents — competition and match data (requested by our server, not your browser)
  • Our AI provider — powers the on-site assistant
  • Discord — only if you link your account or use the club bot
  • Google Maps — only if you press "Load the map" on the contact page
  • MongoDB — the database the site's records are kept in
  • Vercel — hosting and file storage

Each has its own privacy policy, which we recommend reviewing.

5. Data Retention

  • Contact messages are kept until a team admin deletes them.
  • Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter.
  • Rate-limiting records (IP address and timestamp) expire automatically within an hour.
  • The signed-in device list keeps your 20 most recent devices; signing out removes that device.
  • Member account details, member pages, attendance and sign-ups are kept while you are a member, or until you or an admin remove them.

6. Data Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Traffic is served over HTTPS. Member sessions are invalidated when a password or role changes, and repeated failed sign-ins are locked out.

7. Your Choices

Ask us to delete anything you have sent — a contact message, a newsletter subscription, or a member account — and we will remove it. Email requests are fine.

Members can change their own page and its visibility, remove their photo, unlink Discord, and sign out other devices from the account page at any time.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch.
