Who We Are

We are a student-led robotics club competing in the VEX V5 Robotics Competition. Our members learn hands-on mechanical design, electrical engineering, and complex C++ / Python programming.

Through intense competition and collaboration, we push the boundaries of what high school students can achieve in robotics and engineering.

Our teams work year-round to design, build, and program competitive robots that can tackle the season's challenges with precision and creativity.
