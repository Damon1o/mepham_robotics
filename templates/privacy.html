Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what the site needs to work:

  • Contact form: the name, email address, message, and topic you submit. We also record the IP address and browser user-agent string of the submission, which we use to rate-limit spam.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Member accounts: username, email address, role, and a salted bcrypt hash of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Assistant chat: the message you type is sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. We do not store the conversation.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To reply to your enquiries
  • To send newsletter updates you asked for
  • To limit spam and abusive traffic
  • To sign members in to restricted team pages

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We set one cookie: a signed session cookie that keeps you signed in and carries the token that protects forms against cross-site request forgery. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and over HTTPS it is also marked Secure.

We use browser local storage for one thing: your chosen colour theme. It never leaves your device.

We use no tracking or advertising cookies and no analytics scripts.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Fonts — typography
  • Givebutter — donation processing, embedded on the support page
  • RobotEvents — competition and match data (requested by our server, not your browser)
  • Our AI provider — powers the on-site assistant
  • Vercel — hosting and file storage

Each has its own privacy policy, which we recommend reviewing.

5. Data Retention

  • Contact messages are kept until a team admin deletes them.
  • Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter.
  • Rate-limiting records (IP address and timestamp) expire automatically within an hour.

6. Data Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Traffic is served over HTTPS. Member sessions are invalidated when a password or role changes, and repeated failed sign-ins are locked out.

7. Your Choices

Ask us to delete anything you have sent — a contact message, a newsletter subscription, or a member account — and we will remove it. Email requests are fine.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch.
